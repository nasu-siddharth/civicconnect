Small Problems Become Bigger When They're Ignored

Public infrastructure issues directly impact safety, transport, and daily life. Timely reporting helps authorities prioritize fixes before accidents occur.

🛣️

Road Damage

Potholes, cracks, and uneven pavements that damage vehicles and create travel hazards.

💡

Streetlight Problems

Broken or flickering lights leaving roads dark, reducing nighttime pedestrian safety.

🗑️

Garbage

Overflowing bins and illegal dump sites causing hygiene, pest, and health concerns.

🚰

Water Issues

Leaking pipelines, stagnant water puddles, and drainage blocks wasting resources.
